Company Knowledge Base

Leave Policy:
Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year.
Employees must submit leave requests at least 3 working days in advance.

Work From Home Policy:
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Working Hours:
Normal working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

IT Support:
Employees can contact the IT support team for hardware, software and network problems.

Security Policy:
Employees must not share company passwords with other employees.
Company confidential information must not be shared with unauthorized persons.
